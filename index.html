      How to read these results

      • Scanned or image-only PDFs have no readable text. Export your resume from Word, Google Docs, or LaTeX as a PDF, or paste the text into the box on the left.
      • Skill matching uses a fixed list of about 120 common tech and soft skills with their usual spellings (for example "JS" counts as JavaScript). Rare tools may be missed; Claude's review can catch some of these.
      • Exact wording matters to the keyword match. "Built REST endpoints" won't count as "API design". The match can't judge depth or quality.
      • Claude can make mistakes. It is told to use only what's in your resume, and the page checks its quotes against your text, but read every suggestion before you copy it. Fill every [placeholder] with your real numbers or remove it.
      • Never add a skill you don't have just to raise the score. Interviewers ask about everything on your resume.
